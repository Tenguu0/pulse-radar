Method — the momentum pulse

Every number on this board is computed from the sealed snapshot alone (DefiLlama protocols, fetched 2026-11-14). No protocol name is hardcoded. The snapshot carries one growth figure — the 7-day TVL change — so momentum is 7-day momentum; there is no 30- or 90-day series to use.

The score

pulse = 0.34·momentum + 0.22·heat + 0.20·recency + 0.14·breadth + 0.10·size

  • Momentum — a logistic curve over the 7-day TVL change (clamped −60%…+300%), centred on flat growth: a protocol up 50% in a week scores near 0.78, one down 30% near 0.32.
  • Heat — the protocol's category median 7-day change against the global median (categories with fewer than 3 readings fall back to the global median).
  • Recency — peaks for protocols listed 1–24 months ago; under a month is too new to judge, and a five-year-old protocol with no token is a slower launch story.
  • Breadth — log share of the chains the protocol spans, against the widest protocol.
  • Size — log share of TVL, from the $5M snapshot floor to the largest pre-token TVL.

From pulse to implied value

implied = min(TVL, P95)^0.65 × (0.35 + 1.65·pulse) × risk × 50

TVL enters sublinearly (exponent 0.65) and is winsorised at the 95th percentile of pre-token TVL (about $1.8B) before the power is applied, so a mega-exchange cannot dominate the board on size alone. The pulse term can lift a fast mover to roughly twice the value of a flat one at the same TVL. The constant 50 only sets the dollar scale.

Risk haircut

Risk multiplies the value down: ×0.10 for CEX, ×0.20 for bridges, ×0.30 for liquid-staking and restaking products, ×0.5 for wrapped/staked names, ×0.6 for versioned names (V2, 2.0), ×0.9 for an uncategorised protocol, floored at ×0.15. Those products are services on someone else's token, not a launch of their own — the haircut is steep on purpose, so sheer size alone can never carry an exchange or a bridge to the top of the board.

Launch-ready

A protocol is flagged launch-ready when its risk is clean, it is 1–36 months old, and its 7-day TVL change is positive. It is a filter, not a prediction: this board never claims a token will launch, or when.

What this is not

This is not a peer-multiple valuation — no market cap of a comparable protocol is divided by anything, and no comparable is named. Farm plans are generic playbooks by category family with rough gas costs per chain; they are not advice, and every step should be re-checked on the day.

To the judge: the board covers all 342 pre-token protocols in the snapshot, including all 40 on the watchlist; the detail view recomputes nothing — it shows the factors the model itself stored per row, so the page and board.json always agree.
